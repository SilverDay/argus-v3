# USB Scan Station — How to check a USB stick

The station checks USB sticks and USB disks for malware **before** you use them on a
company computer. You do not need to type anything.

## Checking a device

1. **Look at the screen.** It must be blue and say **"Insert a USB device"**.
   If the screen is dark and only shows the time, touch it or simply insert the device.
   If it says **"Scanning unavailable"**, do not use the station: contact IT.
2. **Insert one USB device.** The check starts by itself.
3. **Wait.** While the screen says **"Please wait. Do not remove the device."**, leave the
   device plugged in. Large devices can take several minutes.
4. **Read the result** (table below).
5. **Remove the device only when the screen says "You can now remove the device."**

Insert only one device at a time. Do not use USB hubs or adapters with several ports.

## What the result means

| Screen | Colour | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No known threats detected** | green ✓ | Nothing harmful was found. This is not a guarantee that the device is safe. | Remove the device when told. You may use it as your company rules allow. |
| **Threat detected** | red ✗ | Malware was found. | **Do not use the device.** Remove it when told and give it to IT. |
| **Suspicious content** | orange ⚠ | Something unusual was found. | Do not use the device. Contact IT first. |
| **Not fully checked** | brown ? | Part of the device could not be checked (for example encrypted or damaged files, or an unknown partition). | Treat it as **not checked**. Contact IT. |
| **Scan error** | grey ✗ | The station had a problem during the check. | Contact IT. Follow the removal instructions on the screen. |
| **Device not accepted** | purple ⛔ | This kind of device is not allowed (for example a device that also works as a keyboard, a hub, or a device that is too large). | Remove it. Contact IT if you need this device. |
| **Scanning unavailable** | dark grey | The station is not ready. | Do not insert devices. Contact IT. |
| **Device not safely ejected** | red ⚠ | The station could not finish safely. | **Do not remove the device.** Contact IT. |

If you pull the device out too early, the screen says **"The device was removed before the
check finished."** The device has **not** been checked. Insert it again and wait.

## Good to know

- The station never changes, deletes or copies your files. It only reads them.
- The public screen never shows your file names.
- A keyboard or mouse plugged into the station does nothing. That is intentional.
- Only one device can be checked at a time. A second device is refused.

## Operators only

Trained operators can open a protected menu. Hold the top-right corner of the start screen
for about 3 seconds, then enter your operator PIN. The number pad changes its layout every
time; this is intentional. After several wrong PINs the menu is locked for a while.

The menu shows the last result and the station status, and lets you change the language. If
the site uses result review, the screen says **"Result on hold"**: confirm with your PIN, or
wait for the automatic release.

Never tell anyone your PIN and never write it on the station.
